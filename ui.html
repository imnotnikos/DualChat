Connect your accounts

Each step opens the right page right here in DualChat. Your keys are captured automatically and saved only on this PC.

YTYouTube

  1. Sign in to Google Cloud and create a project
  2. Turn on YouTube Data API v3
  3. Set up Google Auth Platform: any app name, your email, audience External
  4. Add your channel's Google account under Test users
  5. Create a client of type TVs and Limited Input devices. DualChat grabs the keys for you.
Already have keys? Paste them here instead.

TWTwitch

  1. Twitch needs two-factor authentication on your account to register apps
  2. Register an app: any unique name, redirect URL http://localhost, category Chat Bot, client type Public, then Create
  3. Click Manage on your app. DualChat grabs the Client ID for you.
